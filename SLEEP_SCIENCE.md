# Sleep Science: Prevalence, Health Impact & Mental Health

A Python data visualisation project exploring sleep disorders, the health 
risks of sleep deprivation, and the link between poor sleep and mental 
health conditions in the UK.

## Why this matters

Sleep disorders affect millions of people in the UK yet are frequently 
underdiagnosed and undertreated. Poor sleep is linked to serious conditions 
including Type 2 diabetes, heart disease and depression. Understanding 
sleep science is increasingly important in clinical settings across the NHS.

## What the charts show

- Prevalence of sleep disorders in the UK (insomnia, sleep apnoea, 
  restless leg syndrome, narcolepsy)
- Increased health risks associated with chronic sleep deprivation
- Percentage of mental health patients affected by poor sleep
- Recommended vs actual sleep hours across age groups

## Key findings

- 36% of UK adults suffer from insomnia
- Chronic sleep deprivation increases Type 2 diabetes risk by 83%
- 90% of PTSD patients experience poor sleep
- Teenagers get on average 2.5 hours less sleep than recommended
