# Endometriosis & Infertility: Data Visualisation

A Python data visualisation project exploring the relationship between 
endometriosis diagnosis rates and infertility in the UK.

## Why this matters

Endometriosis affects an estimated 1.5 million people in the UK, yet the 
average time to diagnosis is 8 years. This project visualises the real 
impact of delayed diagnosis on patient outcomes.

## What the charts show

- Diagnosed vs undiagnosed endometriosis cases in the UK
- Time to diagnosis breakdown across patients
- Percentage of endometriosis patients experiencing infertility
- Comparison of early vs late diagnosis on patient outcomes

## Key findings

- An estimated 1.3 million people in the UK remain undiagnosed
- 40% of endometriosis patients experience infertility
- Late diagnosis significantly increases risk of infertility, chronic 
  pain, surgery, and mental health issues

## Tools used

- Python 3
- Matplotlib
- Pandas
